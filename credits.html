Music and credits

The shared library uses scores from PDMX, including the existing 300-piece classical and folk selection. The newly imported scores use files marked valid and without an internal license conflict in the source catalogue. Arrangements, instruments and attribution vary; the catalogue is not the whole MuseScore website and inclusion is not a claim that every underlying composition is public domain.

Genre and period labels help browsing. Decades are shown where original-release dates have been identified; otherwise they are marked “Not listed”.

Spotify Basic Pitch and TensorFlow.js provide the recording-to-notes engine (Apache-2.0). Recorded piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Violin and cello: tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus Brosowsky (CC BY 3.0).
