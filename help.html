Find a song. Let’s roll.

Choose a song

  1. Open the Library. Browse the list, search a title or artist, or use the filters.
  2. Tap Open beside a song. Its notes appear above the keyboard.
  3. Tap Play. The coloured notes show the two hands.

Practise at your pace

Open Playback controls to slow the song, choose a hand, or adjust the sound. Use the progress bar to move to another part of the song.

The keyboard fits the song in portrait. To explore the whole piano, choose All 88 keys and drag sideways. Expand gives the player more space.

Upload your own song

Choose Upload a song, then pick a file from Files. MP3, M4A and WAV are good recording choices. MP4 and MOV videos work when the browser can read their audio. MIDI, MusicXML and MXL scores already contain the notes.

A clear piano recording gives the best result. A full band or vocals may produce extra notes.

Your library and privacy

The Song library is shared with everyone. Your uploads and your ten Recently played songs belong to your personal library.

Choose Sign in → Create an account to use a private library across your phone, iPad and computer. Confirm your email, return to this app and sign in. Use the same account on each device. If the confirmation email opens another page, close that page and return here.

Already saved songs in this browser? After signing in, choose Move this library to my account. This is your choice: signing in alone does not copy another person’s browser library into your account.

Your friend should create their own account. They can use the same shared collection, but cannot see your private uploads or recent plays. Sign out when you finish on a shared computer. Without signing in, songs stay in the current browser and do not follow you to other devices.

Private song notes and recordings are stored in a separate account service, never in the public GitHub repository. Recordings up to 50 MB can be retained with the notes. The service uses a free plan with usage limits. Back up your songs also keeps a portable copy; Restore a backup brings it into your selected library.

Add the app to your phone

First open the player in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. The red icon opens the player. You don’t need to rotate your phone.
