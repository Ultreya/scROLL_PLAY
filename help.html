scROLL_PLAY · CODEX

This is the separate Codex edition. Open its published website in Safari.

  1. Tap Safari’s Share button (the square with an upward arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. If necessary, scroll down the Share menu.
  3. Tap Add. Your icon is called scROLL_PLAY.

Open that icon each time to use the same saved-song library.

Play a piece

Open Free classical pieces (300), tap a composer or search a title, then tap Open scroll. Press Play above the keyboard. Use the speed and hand buttons to practise.

Keep your songs

Songs are saved on this device. Use Back up songs to save a portable copy in Files, and Restore backup to bring it back. Safari and a Home Screen app can have separate storage. Removing the app or clearing website data can remove its saved songs.

YouTube and modern songs

YouTube importing is experimental: YouTube can refuse audio access from a browser. Moving to GitHub does not remove that restriction. The modern song catalogue is an availability list, and its scores are not yet connected to the player.
