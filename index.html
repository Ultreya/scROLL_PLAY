Tap the scroll to play or pause. Turn the phone sideways for a wider keyboard. Use the Note length slider, or pinch the scroll with two fingers, to make the notes longer or shorter. Hands are worked out automatically by a small model trained on piano scores where the hands are known. You do not need to set anything. It is right for most notes but not all. If you want to nudge it, press + on Hand split to give more notes to the left hand, or minus to give more to the right. Stray notes far above or below the rest are left off the keyboard. Transpose and the hand buttons change the piano notes, not the original recording.
